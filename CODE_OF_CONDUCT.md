# 🫧 Bubbles Academic Team Code of Conduct

## 🌟 Our Pledge

We as team members, academic collaborators, and advisors pledge to make participation in our academic project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to a professional, inclusive, and productive academic environment focused on creating technology that promotes critical thinking and informed news consumption for our graduation project.

---

## 🎯 Our Standards

### ✅ **Encouraged Behavior**

**🤝 Respectful Communication**
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community and users
- Showing empathy towards other community members

**🧠 Intellectual Integrity**
- Acknowledging sources and contributions appropriately
- Being honest about limitations and uncertainties
- Encouraging critical thinking and diverse perspectives
- Supporting evidence-based discussions
- Maintaining academic and professional integrity

**🤗 Collaborative Spirit**
- Helping newcomers and less experienced contributors
- Sharing knowledge and expertise generously
- Working towards consensus while respecting different opinions
- Contributing constructively to discussions and decisions
- Supporting the team's academic and professional goals

**🌍 Inclusive Environment**
- Using gender-neutral language when uncertain
- Respecting pronouns and identity choices
- Being mindful of cultural differences and perspectives
- Creating space for underrepresented voices
- Promoting accessibility in our tools and communications

### ❌ **Unacceptable Behavior**

**🚫 Harassment and Discrimination**
- Discriminatory jokes, language, or imagery
- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments
- Trolling, inflammatory comments, or deliberate intimidation
- Public or private harassment of any kind
- Publishing others' private information without permission

**📰 Misinformation and Bad Faith**
- Deliberately spreading false or misleading information
- Promoting conspiracy theories or propaganda
- Engaging in bad faith arguments or discussions
- Undermining the project's mission of promoting critical thinking
- Attempting to manipulate news content for personal or political gain

**🔒 Academic Confidentiality**
- Protecting proprietary project information and code
- Respecting intellectual property and non-disclosure agreements
- Maintaining confidentiality of internal discussions and decisions
- Following university policies on academic project confidentiality

**💼 Professional Misconduct**
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Inappropriate use of sexualized language or imagery
- Advocating for or encouraging any harmful behavior
- Spam, commercial solicitation, or off-topic promotion
- Violating confidentiality agreements or NDAs

---

### 🛡️ **Our Responsibilities**

### 👥 **Team Leaders and Academic Supervisors**
Team leaders, academic advisors, and faculty supervisors are responsible for:
- Clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior
- Taking appropriate and fair corrective action for violations
- Moderating discussions and maintaining professional standards
- Protecting intellectual property and academic integrity
- Communicating reasons for disciplinary decisions when appropriate

### 🎓 **Academic Context**
Given our academic setting, additional responsibilities include:
- Upholding Bilkent University's academic integrity standards
- Respecting intellectual property and attribution requirements
- Maintaining professional relationships with advisors and mentors
- Supporting fellow students' learning and development

### 🌐 **Technical Platform**
For our news platform specifically, we must:
- Ensure AI/ML models are developed ethically and transparently
- Respect user privacy and data protection regulations
- Avoid introducing bias into news aggregation or analysis
- Maintain high standards for information accuracy and reliability

## 🚨 Enforcement

### 📢 **Reporting Violations**

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it through these channels:

**🔒 Confidential Reporting**
- **Email**: conduct@bubblesnews.com
- **Project Lead**: @bahadiryavas (if not involved in the incident)
- **Academic Advisor**: Through official university channels
- **Anonymous**: Use anonymous reporting form (link in project documentation)

**📝 What to Include in Reports**
- Date, time, and location of the incident
- Description of what happened
- Names of people involved (if comfortable sharing)
- Any supporting evidence (screenshots, witnesses, etc.)
- How you would like us to respond

### ⚖️ **Enforcement Process**

**1. 📨 Initial Response (Within 24 hours)**
- Acknowledge receipt of the report
- Begin preliminary investigation
- Ensure reporter's safety and comfort

**2. 🔍 Investigation (Within 72 hours)**
- Interview relevant parties confidentially
- Gather evidence and context
- Consult with academic advisors if needed
- Determine if violation occurred

**3. 🎯 Response Actions**
Based on severity and context, responses may include:

**⚠️ Warning**
- Private written warning explaining the violation
- Clarification of expected behavior
- Temporary restriction from certain community spaces

**⏸️ Temporary Suspension**
- Temporary ban from community interactions
- Removal of privileges (repo access, meeting attendance)
- Required completion of educational materials or training

**🔒 Permanent Ban**
- Permanent removal from all community spaces
- Forfeiture of recognition or attribution
- Potential academic misconduct reporting
- Legal action if warranted

**4. 📋 Documentation and Follow-up**
- Document incident and response for future reference
- Follow up with affected parties
- Monitor for retaliation or repeated violations
- Adjust policies if needed

## 🎓 Academic Integrity Specifics

### 📚 **Research and Development Ethics**
- All research methodologies must be transparent and reproducible
- AI/ML models must be tested for bias and fairness
- User data must be handled according to privacy regulations
- External code or resources must be properly attributed

### 🤝 **Collaboration Standards**
- Credit all contributors fairly and accurately
- Respect intellectual property of team members and external parties
- Maintain confidentiality of unpublished work and sensitive data
- Support team members' individual academic and career goals

### 📊 **Data and Algorithm Ethics**
- Ensure news data sources are legitimate and ethically obtained
- Implement bias detection and mitigation in AI systems
- Respect user privacy and consent in all data collection
- Maintain transparency about algorithmic decision-making

---

## 🌐 Special Considerations for News Platform

### 📰 **Information Integrity**
- Never knowingly promote false or misleading information
- Maintain political neutrality in platform design and implementation
- Respect diverse viewpoints while promoting critical thinking
- Implement safeguards against manipulation and propaganda

### 🤖 **AI Ethics**
- Ensure AI systems promote rather than undermine media literacy
- Regularly audit algorithms for bias and fairness
- Be transparent about AI limitations and potential errors
- Respect user agency in news consumption and interpretation

### 🔒 **Privacy and Trust**
- Protect user reading patterns and preferences
- Implement strong data security and privacy controls
- Be transparent about data collection and usage
- Respect user rights to data portability and deletion
